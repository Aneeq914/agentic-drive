# Proposal: Acme Retail Website Redesign

**Proposal No.:** BL-P-2026-011
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Prepared for:** Laura Bennett, Head of Digital, Acme Retail
**Prepared by:** Saad Mirza (Account Manager) and Zara Sheikh (Product Manager), Brightleaf Labs
**Valid until:** 2026-08-19

## 1. The problem
Most of Acme Retail's shoppers now use their phones, but the site was built for desktop. Mobile visitors convert at about half the desktop rate. One in five searches returns nothing, and every content change needs a developer. With Black Friday ahead, each week of delay costs sales.

## 2. What we propose
A mobile-first redesign of the storefront in 15 weeks, live before Black Friday:
- A new homepage, category pages and product detail pages built on Acme's refreshed brand
- **Search v2**: forgives typos, understands synonyms and local product names, and uses faceted filters
- A move from WordPress to a headless CMS, so Acme's team can edit pages on its own
- A visual reskin of the existing checkout (the payment flow itself is unchanged)
