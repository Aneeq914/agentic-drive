# Q3 2026 Customer Churn Report — Retainer Clients

Prepared by: Saad Mirza (Account Manager)
Reviewed by: Zara Sheikh (Product Manager), Omar Siddiqui (Finance)
Date: 2026-10-02
Period: 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30

## Summary
Two of our nine retainer clients left in Q3. Logo churn was 22.2% and gross revenue churn was 13.3%, the worst quarter since we started retainers in 2024. Project revenue (Acme Retail, Northwind Health) made up for the loss in total revenue. Retainers, though, are our steadiest income, and the warning signs for both losses were visible weeks in advance.

## Key numbers
| Metric | Start of Q3 | End of Q3 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retainer clients | 9 | 7 | −2 |
| Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) | $38,400 | $33,300 | −$5,100 |
| Average retainer size | $4,267 | $4,757 | +11.5% |

Retainer revenue recognised in Q3 was $107,350, against $115,200 if no client had left.

## Clients lost
**Pixelport Games** ($2,750/month, design support, client since Mar 2025). Ended on Jul 31. They hired two in-house designers after their Series A and cut their agency spend. In the exit call they said our turnaround times were fine, but they wanted designers sitting with their game teams.

**Saffron Foods** ($2,350/month, social media content, client since Nov 2024). Paused on Sep 1 under the decision from the Aug 21 finance sync, after INV-0868 ($2,350) went unpaid for more than 30 days. Their marketing manager left in June, and nobody on their side owned the relationship afterwards. The invoice has been provisioned as a bad debt.

## What the losses had in common
- Both clients' main contact changed or left in the 60 days before they churned.
- Neither had a quarterly review with us in 2026.
- We noticed the risk only after money was involved (an overdue invoice, a notice period).

## Clients at risk now
| Client | MRR | Signal | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lahore Literary Festival | $3,100 | Scope shrinking two months in a row | Saad |
| Tandoor & Co. | $4,200 | New CMO, no meeting since July | Ayesha Malik |

## Recommendations
1. Start a monthly retainer health check (on the Q4 roadmap, owned by Saad).
2. Hold a quarterly business review with every retainer client, with Ayesha joining for clients worth over $4,000/month.
3. Send automatic payment reminders (the invoicing tool rollout, owned by Omar) so that late payments show up within a week.
4. Target: retainer churn under 5% in Q4, which means losing no more than one client.
