# Master Services Agreement

**Agreement No.:** BL-MSA-2026-007
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-04

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is between **Brightleaf Labs (Pvt.) Ltd.**, Office 3, Level 2, The Hive Coworking, DHA Phase 5, Lahore ("Brightleaf"), and **Acme Retail Ltd.**, 88-B Industrial Area, Kot Lakhpat, Lahore ("Client"). Each is a "Party".

## 1. Services
1.1 Brightleaf will provide design and software development services described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") signed by both Parties. Each SOW is governed by this Agreement.
1.2 The first SOW, **SOW-01: Website Redesign**, is attached as Schedule A. It is based on proposal BL-P-2026-011 dated 2026-07-20.

## 2. Fees and Payment Terms
2.1 Fees for SOW-01 are fixed at **$48,000** (forty-eight thousand US dollars) and are invoiced in milestones:

| Milestone | Trigger | Share | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Signature of this Agreement | 30% | $14,400 |
| M2 | Client sign-off on final designs | 40% | $19,200 |
| M3 | Production launch | 30% | $14,400 |

2.2 Brightleaf invoices each milestone when it is reached. Payment is due **thirty (30) days** after the invoice date (Net 30), by bank transfer to the account stated on the invoice.
2.3 Overdue amounts carry a late fee of **1.5% per month**. If an undisputed invoice is more than 45 days overdue, Brightleaf may suspend work after giving 7 days' written notice.
2.4 Change requests are billed at **$600 per person-day**, but only after the Client approves them in writing.
2.5 The Client reimburses approved third-party costs (fonts, stock photography, SaaS licences) at cost. Each item over $500 needs written approval first.

## 3. Intellectual Property
3.1 When an invoice is paid in full, the Client owns all deliverables created for it under the SOW.
3.2 Brightleaf keeps its pre-existing tools, libraries and know-how. The Client receives a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use them as part of the deliverables.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each Party will keep the other's confidential information secret during this Agreement and for three (3) years after it ends.

## 5. Warranty and Liability
5.1 Brightleaf will fix defects reported within 30 days of launch at no charge.
5.2 Each Party's total liability is limited to the fees paid under the relevant SOW in the previous twelve (12) months.

## 6. Term and Termination
6.1 This Agreement runs for 24 months from the Effective Date.
6.2 Either Party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. The Client pays for all work done up to the termination date.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan. The courts at Lahore have exclusive jurisdiction.
