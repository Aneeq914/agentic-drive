# Job Description: Senior Backend Engineer

**Company:** Brightleaf Labs, a 14-person product studio
**Location:** Lahore, Gulberg III (hybrid: Tuesday to Thursday in the office)
**Reports to:** Daniyal Qureshi, Engineering Lead
**Employment type:** Full-time, permanent
**Salary range:** PKR 650,000 – 850,000 per month, plus an annual bonus of up to 10%
**Posted:** 2026-09-08 · **Apply by:** 2026-10-09 · **Target start date:** 2026-11-16
**Requisition:** REQ-015

## About us
Brightleaf Labs designs and builds websites, apps and internal tools for retailers, healthcare providers and startups across Pakistan and the Gulf. We are small on purpose: every engineer talks to clients, shapes the product and owns what they ship.

## The role
You will be the third backend engineer on a team of six, alongside Fatima and Rabia. In your first six months you will lead the backend of a patient-facing mobile app for a healthcare client: appointment booking, lab results and reminders, integrated with their existing clinic system. You will also help run the search platform we are launching for a large retail client in November.

## What you'll do
- Design and build REST APIs in Node.js (TypeScript) with PostgreSQL
- Integrate with third-party systems (HL7 FHIR or similar health APIs, payment gateways, inventory systems)
- Own reliability: monitoring, alerting, backups and incident follow-ups
- Review code and mentor two mid-level engineers
- Join client workshops to turn messy requirements into clear technical plans
- Write the specs we work from (see how we write them at brightleaf.example/engineering)

## What you'll bring
- 5+ years of backend development, with at least 2 years running production systems
- Strong SQL and data-modelling skills. You know when an index helps and when it hurts.
- Experience handling sensitive data: access control, audit logs, encryption at rest
- Clear written English. Most of our clients read our specs before they meet us.

## Nice to have
- Healthcare or fintech compliance experience
- Search engines (OpenSearch or Elasticsearch)
- Experience in an agency or studio, juggling two clients at once

## Benefits
- 24 days of paid leave plus public holidays
- Health insurance for you, your spouse and your children
- A monthly internet allowance and a one-time home-office stipend (see the remote work policy)
- An annual learning budget of PKR 150,000
