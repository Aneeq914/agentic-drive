# Acme Retail Website Redesign — Kickoff Call Notes

Date: 2026-08-11 (Tuesday, 15:00–16:15, video call)
Brightleaf Labs: Ayesha Malik (CEO), Zara Sheikh (Product Manager), Daniyal Qureshi (Engineering Lead), Mehwish Javed (Product Designer), Saad Mirza (Account Manager)
Acme Retail: Laura Bennett (Head of Digital), Kamran Aziz (E-commerce Manager), Hira Saleem (Brand Manager)
Notes by: Zara Sheikh

## Agenda
1. Introductions and project goals
2. Where the current site falls short
3. Scope, timeline and ways of working
4. Next steps

## Discussion
Laura opened by saying the redesign is Acme's top digital priority for the year and has to be live before Black Friday.

Kamran shared numbers from the current site: 62% of traffic is on mobile, but mobile converts at 1.4% compared with 2.9% on desktop. On-site search returns no results for about 18% of queries, mostly because of misspellings and local product names.

Hira said the brand refresh is finished and the new guidelines are ready. She asked that every page design be checked against them.

Zara went through the scope from proposal BL-P-2026-011 and SOW-01 under the MSA: new homepage, category and product pages, a new product search (search v2) and a reskin of the checkout. Rebuilding the checkout itself is out of scope.

Daniyal proposed moving product content from the current WordPress setup to a headless CMS (Strapi). Acme's team would then be able to edit pages without a developer. Kamran agreed, as long as the 18,000 SKUs stay in their existing inventory system.

## Decisions
- Go-live date: 2026-11-20, one week before Black Friday.
- Success metric: mobile conversion of 2.2% or more by the end of Q1 2027.
- Design sign-off: Sep 18. Content freeze: Nov 6.
- A weekly status call every Thursday at 16:00. Saad owns the status report.
