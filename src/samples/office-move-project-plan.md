# Office Move — Project Plan

Owner: Usman Tariq (Ops & HR)
Sponsor: Ayesha Malik (CEO)
Status: Complete (closed 2026-09-04)
From: The Hive Coworking, DHA Phase 5 (12 hot desks, sharing a meeting room)
To: Plot 42, Main Boulevard, Gulberg III, Lahore (2,400 sq ft, our own floor)

## Why we are moving
We have outgrown the co-working space. With 14 people and two more hires planned, we are short of desks, we can't host client workshops (the Northwind discovery sessions were held at their office) and the meeting room is double-booked most days.

## Goals
- Move with no more than one lost working day.
- Keep the total budget at or under $10,500.
- Have the board room ready for client workshops by Sep 1.

## Risks (at planning time)
- The ISP install could slip. We would keep 4G dongles as a backup for the first week.
- The furniture could arrive late. Chairs from The Hive could be borrowed for up to 3 days.

## Lessons learned
- Order internet first. The second ISP took 26 days.
- Moving on a Saturday meant we lost no client time.
- Total spend came in $630 under budget, mostly because the movers and the networking cost less than quoted.
