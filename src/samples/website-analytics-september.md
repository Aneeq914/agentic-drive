## What happened
- Newsletter sign-ups dipped because the Summer Switch campaign wound down in mid-September. Q3 as a whole still beat its goal (see the Q3 marketing wrap-up).
- The invoicing article proves that practical "how we run the studio" posts bring in the right audience: 9 of the 37 leads read it first.
- The careers page traffic came mostly from LinkedIn and the Lahore developer Slack communities.
- Paid social spend stayed flat compared with August, so the growth in social traffic was organic.
