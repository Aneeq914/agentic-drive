# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Policy owner:** Usman Tariq (Ops & HR)
**Approved by:** Ayesha Malik (CEO)
**Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 2026-09-01 · **Next review:** 2027-03-01
**Applies to:** all Brightleaf Labs employees. Contractors follow their contract.

## 1. Why this policy exists
Since we moved to the Gulberg office on Aug 31, 2026, we have a space built for working together. This policy explains when we come in, when we don't, and what Brightleaf provides so remote days work well. It replaces version 1.3 (2024), written when we shared desks at The Hive.

## 2. Our hybrid model
- **Office days:** Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are in-office days for everyone on a hybrid pattern.
- **Flexible days:** on Monday and Friday you may work from home or from the office.
- **Core hours:** 11:00–16:00 PKT, every working day, wherever you are. Outside core hours, plan your own time.
- **Client workshops:** if a client session is booked on a flexible day, the people involved attend in person.

## 3. Fully remote arrangements
- Fully remote roles need written approval from the CEO and are noted in the headcount register (currently one role).
- Hybrid employees may work fully remote for up to **10 working days a year**, for example during a family emergency or a visit to another city, with 1 week's notice to their manager.

## 4. Equipment and allowances
- Every employee gets a company laptop. Personal devices may only be used for email and chat.
- **Internet allowance:** PKR 6,000 per month, paid with salary.
- **Home-office stipend:** a one-time PKR 40,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, claimed with a receipt within 3 months of joining or of this policy taking effect.
- Power backup (UPS) for home routers can be reimbursed up to PKR 25,000 every 3 years.

## 5. Security and client data
- Always use the company VPN when you work outside the office.
- Never download client data to personal devices, and never work on it on public Wi-Fi without the VPN. This is strictly enforced for healthcare clients such as Northwind Health under our NDA.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away, including at home.
- Report a lost or stolen device to Usman within 2 hours.

## 6. Availability and communication
- Keep your Slack status up to date. Reply to messages in #client channels within 2 working hours during core hours.
- Cameras are expected on for client calls and optional for internal meetings.
- The weekly engineering standup (Monday 10:00) and the monthly all-hands are attended live.

## 7. Breaches
If this policy is not followed repeatedly, your manager will first have a conversation with you. After that, flexible days may be withdrawn for a period agreed with HR. Security breaches are handled under the information security policy.

Questions go to Usman Tariq at people@brightleaf.example.
